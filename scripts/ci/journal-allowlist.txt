# Regexes (extended) for journal -p err lines that are expected/benign in the
# qemu 'virt' boot environment. EVERY entry needs a comment saying why.

# qemu 'virt' has no Pi firmware interface — vcgencmd/firmware errors are
# expected off-hardware and covered by the hardware release checklist
vchiq|vcgencmd|raspberrypi-firmware

# no sound hardware exists in the CI VM; audio device probing may error
snd_|alsa.*(no soundcards|not found)

# no i2c bus in the CI VM; ovos-i2csound probing is hardware-only
i2c.*(no such|not found)
